The Henosis Story

Where rarity
meets artistry

Henosis — derived from the Greek word for unity and oneness — was born from a belief that jewellery should be more than an accessory. It should be a singular expression of identity.

Each piece in our collection is meticulously crafted and, in many cases, exists as the only one of its kind in the world. When you wear Henosis, you wear something that belongs to you alone.

We use the finest imitation materials, precision-crafted to mirror the beauty of precious gems and metals — making luxury accessible without compromising on elegance or exclusivity.
