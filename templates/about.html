Our Story

Born from a belief that beauty should be accessible — and that every woman deserves to feel singular.

"Henosis"

From the ancient Greek: a word meaning oneness, unity, the state of being whole. We named our brand after this concept because we believe every piece of jewellery should speak to the wholeness of the person who wears it.

Henosis was founded with a singular vision — to create luxury imitation jewellery so exquisite that the world cannot distinguish it from the real thing. Not through deception, but through artistry.

The Philosophy

Rarity as a value

Many of our pieces are crafted only once. A single mold. A single vision. A single owner. When you purchase a "One of a Kind" piece from Henosis, you hold something that will never exist again.

This is our commitment: to the woman who refuses to blend in. Who understands that true luxury is not about price — it is about exclusivity of spirit.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Exclusivity

Each piece is created with intention. Many exist only once. We believe scarcity is the purest form of luxury.

Craftsmanship

Every detail is considered. From the setting to the finish, Henosis pieces reflect meticulous attention to artistry.

Accessibility

Luxury should not require compromise. We make beautiful, high-quality jewellery accessible to every woman.
